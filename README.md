# MSLFusion

# MSLFusion is a deep learning - based infrared and visible image fusion project based on the Densefuse network.  

# The innovative part is the integration strategy.

# It is worth noting that some content will be updated in a timely manner after the paper is accepted.

# Comparisons of our method with other approaches on the TNO and MFFW2 datasets are provided in the Example folder. 
Specifically, Figures 1 and 2 present comparisons of fusion results regarding detail preservation and performance in smoke scenarios across multiple methods on the TNO dataset. 
Figures 3 and 4 display comparisons of artifact generation and color retention among various methods on the MFFW2 dataset. 
Figure 5 shows the comparison of results for the downstream visual task—object detection—conducted on YOLOv5s.
